EasyCube — Privacy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Nothing is collected

EasyCube has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Nobody is counting how often you open it or what you look at, and nothing is sold, shared or handed to anyone.

Your progress stays yours

The cases you have learned, your times and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is switched on, they also travel through your own iCloud account so your other devices see them. That is Apple's storage, under your control, and it is not readable by the app's developer.

Feedback, if you send any

The app has a feedback screen. It sends exactly what you typed, plus the app's version and the name and version of the system you run it on, so that a bug report can be acted on. No name, no email address and no device identifier is attached. Sending is entirely optional; the app works the same if you never do.

The same text is in the app, under Settings → Privacy.
